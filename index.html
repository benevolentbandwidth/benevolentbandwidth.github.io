The Benevolent Bandwidth Foundation

AI for Nonprofits

We build AI-powered tools and platforms that help nonprofits and NGOs do more with less. We also educate nonprofit leaders and teams to understand, adopt, and stay current with AI so they can stay focused on what matters most: their mission.

Our Mission

We build AI tools and platforms exclusively for nonprofits and NGOs, and we educate their teams to adopt and stay current with AI -- helping them automate operations, amplify impact, and serve more people with the resources they have.

Our Vision

A world where every nonprofit has access to the same AI capabilities as the world's largest organizations -- and the expertise to use them effectively.

The b² Principles

  • Problem First: We build useful tools that are practical, usable, easy to adopt. We start with the problem not the solution or technology
  • Privacy Non-negotiable: We never collect personal data, no tracking, and we never sell or share data
  • Public Benefit: We use AI for public benefit, prioritizing safety over speed, focus on fairness, and real-world positive impact on humans
  • Open Source by Default: Code, docs, and decisions are transparent
  • Neutrality: We are neutral. We do not support any political positions, candidates, agendas, or ideologies
  • Humble & Curious: We are humble, love learning and are genuinely curious. We invite feedback, own mistakes, and pivot when evidence points to a new direction
  • Inclusive Community: We are an open and inclusive community. We are open to anyone who wants to contribute. We provide support and stand on each other's shoulders
  • Source Ideas Anywhere: We source ideas from anywhere as long as it adheres to the principles above

Our Approach

Trillions of dollars are flowing into AI. Yet the organizations doing the most important work in the world -- nonprofits, NGOs, community programs -- are largely being left behind. They lack the resources, the technical talent, and the infrastructure to adopt AI on their own terms.

We exist to change that. The Benevolent Bandwidth Foundation builds AI tools and platforms specifically for the nonprofit sector. We don't consult -- we build, deploy, and stand behind what we create.

Our approach is grounded in three commitments:

  • Build with, not for: We partner deeply with the nonprofits we serve, embedding ourselves in their operations to understand what they actually need -- not what we assume they need
  • Practical over perfect: We ship tools that work today, in the channels and workflows nonprofits already use, and we iterate from there
  • Open by default: We share our work openly so the entire sector benefits -- every tool, every lesson, every failure

We are not building AI for AI's sake. We are deploying AI engineers and researchers to solve real operational problems in marketing, volunteer management, fundraising, communications, and program delivery -- so nonprofit leaders can spend less time on administration and more time on impact.

Our Work

Core Projects

b2 Agentic OS Platform

Problem domain: Nonprofit Operations

The b2 Agentic OS is an open-source AI platform purpose-built for nonprofits and NGOs. It automates the operations that consume nonprofit staff -- marketing, volunteer management, donor communications, program workflows, and more. The platform runs over the channels organizations already use, speaks the languages their communities speak, and is designed from the ground up to be private, extensible, and humanitarian by default. Any NGO, aid organization, or community program can deploy AI workflows and agents without a technical team. For our first deployment, we are partnering with GiveLight.org to bring this platform to life in the field.

AI Academy for Nonprofits

Problem domain: Education & Capacity Building

The AI Academy for Nonprofits is a hands-on program designed for executive directors, founders, and organizational leaders who want to put AI to work -- today. No technical background required. In just a few hours, participants go from understanding how AI actually works to building live automations, writing better prompts, and leaving with a prioritized AI strategy tailored to their organization. Every session is practical, grounded in real nonprofit challenges, and every participant walks away with something they can use immediately. We work directly with cohorts of nonprofit leaders and also partner with foundations and intermediaries to deliver the program at scale.

Custom AI Solutions

Problem domain: Bespoke Nonprofit Needs

Some nonprofit challenges don't fit a general platform. For organizations with specific, high-impact needs, we deploy AI engineers and researchers to build custom solutions end-to-end -- from problem scoping and data strategy through to deployment and handoff. We work selectively with nonprofits where AI can create significant operational or mission leverage, and where we can build something that lasts. If you're a nonprofit leader with a problem that AI might solve, we'd like to hear from you.

Sample Projects

Orphan Identification Agent for GiveLight

Problem domain: Humanitarian Aid

GiveLight.org sponsors orphaned and vulnerable children in conflict and poverty-affected regions. Identifying children who qualify for sponsorship -- and verifying their status -- is an intensely manual, document-heavy process prone to delay and error. We built an agentic AI system that ingests case files, field reports, and registration documents to identify and prioritize candidates for sponsorship, dramatically reducing the time between a child's need and the support they receive.

Research Agent

Problem domain: Access to Information

Too many important papers get lost in the noise. Most researchers and practitioners cannot reliably scan what is new recently in their area, find truly promising work, and trust that they did not miss something big. This agent helps with this problem by finding, ranking, and explaining recent AI papers on arxiv.org.

EcoTag: The Garment Emissions Scanner

Problem domain: Environmental Impact

EcoTag is a smartphone app that empowers consumers to make sustainable choices by revealing the hidden environmental cost of their clothing. Using Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to scan garment tags, the tool calculates estimated lifecycle carbon emissions based on materials and origin. This provides shoppers with immediate, actionable data on the carbon footprint of their potential purchases.

Delivery Optimizer

Problem domain: Logistics

The Delivery Optimizer helps local businesses like florists and dry cleaners eliminate the waste of manual route planning. Owners simply upload a list of addresses to the web tool, which uses open-source algorithms to generate the most efficient delivery order. This streamlined process saves time and fuel while reducing the carbon footprint of local logistics.

The Revive-or-Recycle Scanner

Problem domain: Environmental Impact

People don't know if fixing old gadgets is worth the cost, so e-waste accumulates. This economic triage tool queries live marketplaces for part and resale prices to advise consumers whether to repair or recycle their old or broken electronics, helping reduce environmental waste while saving money.
