Why Join

Join The Benevolent Bandwidth Foundation and make a meaningful impact while building your career

For Contributors

Build Your Portfolio with Purpose

Ship production code alongside leaders from Meta, Google, Tesla, Amazon, Fidelity, etc. and solve real problems that matter.

What You Get:

  • Real-World Experience: Work on live tools serving actual users - from design, build, deploy to go to market
  • "Open Source Fellow" Title: Add a professional credential to your LinkedIn and resume that signals serious contribution
  • Expert Code Reviews: Get feedback from senior engineers at top tech companies

Top Contributors:

  • Recommendations: Endorsements from industry veterans highlighting your specific contributions
  • Career Mentorship: Access to coaching with Foundation and Technical Advisors
  • Author Credit: Publish blog posts and technical write-ups under your name
  • Network Connections: Advisors may be able to facilitate introductions at companies where they have relationships when there is a good fit
  • References: Strong references from senior practitioners for job applications
Get Involved

For Technical Advisors

(Engineers, Scientists, Product Managers, or Designers, UI/UX)

Lead. Mentor. Network.

You're already great at building. Come practice being a leader/GM with a focus on end to end delivery. Architect solutions, mentor teams, and connect with VPs and Directors from major tech firms and top universities.

What You Get:

  • Leadership Credentials: Use the title "Technical Advisor" on LinkedIn and your resume to demonstrate leadership experience
  • Executive Network Access: Roundtables with Foundation Advisors (VPs, Directors, Professors) for off-the-record industry chats and career discussions
  • Speaking Opportunities: We'll connect you with universities, conferences, and meetups looking for technical speakers
  • Executive Coaching: 1-on-1 career strategy sessions with Foundation Advisors (e.g., "transitioning from IC to management")
  • Talent Pipeline Access: First look at b²'s vetted contributors when you're hiring for your day job

Note: All mentorship, recommendations, and introductions are provided at the discretion of individual advisors based on quality of contribution and availability. b² facilitates connections but does not guarantee employment outcomes.
